Account privacy

Delete your ArangCada account

Confirm your request

Confirming sends your request to our privacy team. Your account remains active while we review and process it. If you did not request deletion, close this page.

In the app, open Profile → Settings → Delete account. We will email a confirmation link to your account address. The link expires after 24 hours. Confirming it sends your request to our privacy team.

If you no longer have the app, email privacy@arangcada.app from the address on your account. We will verify that you own it before processing the request.

What happens next

We review active rides and any trip, complaint, or safety records that the LGU or our privacy policy requires us to keep. We then delete the account and personal data that are no longer needed, de-identify retained records where possible, and email you when processing is complete. Your account remains active until then. We respond within 15 business days.

See our Privacy Policy for retention periods and contact details.
