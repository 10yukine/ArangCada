Account privacy

Delete your ArangCada account

In the app, open Profile → Settings → Delete account. No app? Sign in below instead. Either way, your account is deleted right away.

What happens

Deleted right away: your account and sign-in; your name, photo, email and mobile number; discount claims and ID photos; your chat messages and voice notes; and, for drivers, the driver record and documents.

Kept without your name: past trips, ratings, complaints and SOS reports, for the periods in our Privacy Policy. They can no longer be linked to you.

You cannot delete your account during a ride. Administrator accounts are removed from the admin console.

Delete with your sign-in

Forgot your password? Reset it in the app, or email privacy@arangcada.app from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.
