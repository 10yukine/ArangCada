01 / FOR COMMUTERS

Less figuring it out.
More getting there.

A straightforward journey, from your first search to your trip receipt.

  1. 01

    Choose your destination.

    Search for your stop and check your pickup point. Make sure your driver knows where to meet you.

  2. 02

    Review your ride.

    See your trip details, passenger count, and fare before requesting an Espesyal ride.

  3. 03

    Meet your driver.

    Follow the trip status and keep in touch through the app. Your receipt keeps the details together afterward.

02 / FOR DRIVERS & TODAs

Local knowledge.
Better coordination.

Built around the people who keep Calamba moving.

Drivers receive ride requests and manage their trips in one place. LGU and TODA administrators have a dedicated console for driver records, trip oversight, and feedback.

Open the admin console ↗

Console access requires an assigned administrator account.

03 / THE PROJECT

A small project.
A very local purpose.

ArangCada is an academic capstone project exploring tricycle booking and dispatch in Calamba City. We’re testing with feedback from drivers and the people who use the system.

We’re still in beta. Availability and features may change as testing continues.
