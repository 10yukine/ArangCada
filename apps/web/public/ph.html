For drivers & TODAs

Local knowledge. Better coordination.

Built around the people who keep Calamba moving — and the offices that look after them.

  • Drivers

    Go online, receive nearby ride requests, and manage each trip from pickup to drop-off.

  • TODA officers

    Keep driver records current and follow up on feedback from their terminal.

  • City administrators

    Verify drivers, oversee trips, and respond to safety reports across Calamba.

Before your first ride

A few things to know.

Getting around should feel simple. Here’s where we are today.

Can I book a ride now?

Not yet. ArangCada is in beta testing with local drivers and commuters. Public booking is still closed. Check app availability.

How is my fare calculated?

The app uses Calamba City’s approved fare matrix. You review the fare before requesting your ride, with no surge pricing.

Who verifies the drivers?

LGU and TODA administrators review drivers and their required documents before they can go online.

The project

A small project.
A very local purpose.

ArangCada is an academic capstone project exploring tricycle booking and dispatch in Calamba City. We’re testing with feedback from drivers, TODAs, and the people who ride with them.

  1. Done

    Planned around Calamba’s rules

  2. Done

  3. Now

    Beta testing

    The first Android test build is out. We’re testing with local drivers and commuters.

  4. Next

    Evaluation

    A quality evaluation (ISO/IEC 25010) and feedback from participating drivers.
